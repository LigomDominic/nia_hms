Voice typing (speech to text)

Adds a microphone button to the complaint, notes and diagnosis boxes, so doctors and nurses can dictate instead of typing. It works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge with an internet connection, and the browser sends the audio to its speech service to convert it. Open the app on http://127.0.0.1:5000 or over https, otherwise the browser blocks the microphone.
