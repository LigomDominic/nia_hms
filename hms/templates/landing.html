The patient journey

Every step is tracked, and nothing moves forward until it's paid for.

Cashier registers

Patient data captured, card fee collected, unique ID issued and a doctor assigned.

Doctor consults

Face-to-face consultation; nurse vitals on screen; tests and drugs requested.

Cashier collects

Test and drug bills come to the cashier — or are paid instantly from the e-wallet.

Lab & Pharmacy

Paid tests go to the lab; paid prescriptions go to the pharmacy for dispensing.

Results delivered

Results post back to the doctor and to the patient by email or manual collection.

Patient E-Wallet

Saving towards surgery or ongoing treatment? Patients can fund their wallet in advance — in any amount, at any time. Whenever a new service is billed (drugs, tests, consultation), the system automatically deducts it from the wallet. If the wallet only covers part, the rest goes to the cashier.
